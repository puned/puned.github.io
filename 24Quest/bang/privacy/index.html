The Game's services are provided from the following locations or channels:

We make reasonable efforts to keep this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, app store information and the actual provision of services consistent with each other.

2. Information We Collect

We follow the principles of lawfulness, fairness and necessity, and only process information needed to run the Game, maintain the service, ensure security, serve advertising, perform analytics and meet compliance requirements. Device model, operating system version, app version, system language, time zone, network type, IP address, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, install source, device runtime status and log information.

2.2 Gameplay Information

Level progress, scores, item usage, ad impression records, tap records, session duration, feature usage records, crash logs and diagnostic information.

2.3 Advertising and Analytics Information

Ad requests, ad impressions, ad clicks, ad placements, ad fill status, analytics events, coarse region information and anti-fraud or security verification information.

2.4 Information You Provide

If you contact us through the app store, in-game feedback channels or other public channels, we may process the contact details, problem descriptions, screenshots, device information or other information you voluntarily submit as needed to handle your request.

2.5 Information We Do Not Actively Collect

Unless you voluntarily provide it or applicable law requires otherwise, we do not ask for your real name, government ID numbers, precise geolocation, contacts, photo albums, text messages, call records or other sensitive information unrelated to the Game's core features.
